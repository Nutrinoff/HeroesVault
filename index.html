Nexus Academy

Core concepts, strategy guides, and advanced gameplay guides

Experience & Soaking

Minion death XP is the lifeblood of HotS. Ensure every minion wave in all lanes is soaked during the early game. Missing a wave early can easily give the opposing team a pivotal level advantage for objective fights.

Talent Tier Advantages

Never engage 4v5 or when down a major talent tier (especially levels 10 and 20). Level advantages grant immense stat buffs and game-changing heroic abilities that heavily swing team fights.

Mercenary Timing

Capture siege and bruiser camps right before objectives spawn. This forces the enemy team to split attention between defending structures and contesting the map objective.

Team Composition Balance

A balanced composition typically relies on a reliable Tank, a Healer, a Bruiser or Offlaner, a Ranged Assassin, and a flexible slot for waveclear or multi-class utility.
